# Privacy policy for Android Units

Last updated: 23 September 2026

## The short version

**Units doesn't collect anything about you.** It has no account, no ads, no
analytics, no crash reporter, no tracking of any kind. It doesn't even have
permission to use the network.

## What Units keeps, and where

Units stores two things, both in its own private storage on your device:

- **Your calculation history** — the "have" and "want" expressions and the
  result, so you can scroll back and re-edit an earlier calculation.
- **How often you've used each unit** — so that the units you actually use
  float to the top of the auto-complete list.

That's it. Nothing is uploaded, nothing is shared, and nobody but you and the
app can read it. Clearing the history (menu → Clear history) deletes the
history. Uninstalling Units, or clearing its data in Android's app settings,
deletes everything.

## Backups

If you have Android's backup feature turned on, Android may include Units'
data in your device backup, along with every other app's. That backup goes to
your own Google account and is governed by
[Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy), not this one.
You can turn it off in your device's backup settings.

## Children

There's nothing here to collect, from anyone, of any age.

## Changes

If this ever changes I'll update this page and say so in the release notes.
Since Units is free software, you can also just read the source and check for
yourself.
