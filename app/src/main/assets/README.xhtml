Units is a unit-aware calculator that also does plain unit conversions. It's an Android interpretation of the classic GNU Units utility, and uses Units in Java for the heavy lifting.

Using it

Put what you're converting from in you have (say, 5ft + 6in) and what you want it in into you want (cm). Leave you want empty to get a definition in base units instead.

Both boxes take full expressions, so 3m × 1m × 2cm into liter works, as does 1cup sugar ÷ 0.5gallon into gram ÷ 8floz. Both boxes auto-complete, ordered by how often you've used each unit. Press unit to browse or search the whole list.

If you ask for something that only makes sense as a reciprocal — say 100mpg into liter ÷ 100km — Units works it out from 1 ÷ (100mpg) and tells you it did.

Your data

Units has no network access. Your calculation history and unit-usage counts are stored on your device only. Read the full privacy policy at staticfree.info/projects/units/privacy/.
